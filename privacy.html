8. Opt-out preference signals

We honour the Global Privacy Control (GPC) and the older Do Not Track setting. If your browser or an extension sends either signal, our analytics does not load at all for your visit: no tracking script runs, no cookie is set, and no usage data is collected.

We treat a GPC signal as a valid opt-out of sale and of processing for targeted advertising, as required in Texas since 1 January 2025 and in a growing number of other states. You do not need to submit anything to us for it to take effect, and we do not require an account to honour it.

GPC is available in browsers including Firefox and Brave, and through extensions in others. Because the signal is set per browser and device, you will need to enable it on each.

9. Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies are small text files a website stores on your device. We also use similar technologies such as pixels and local storage. This section lists every cookie this website is capable of setting.

This site sets no cookies at all until analytics loads — and analytics does not load if your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal (see section 8). If you browse with GPC enabled, nothing in the analytics table below is ever placed on your device.

Cookies we set

NameSet byPurposeTypeExpires

That is the complete list. We set no advertising, retargeting or social media cookies, and we do not use pixels from advertising networks or data brokers.

Cookies third parties may set

The consultation booking calendar is provided by Cal.com and loads inside a frame on our booking page. Cal.com may set its own cookies within that frame, governed by Cal.com's privacy policy rather than this one. Those cookies are set only if you scroll to the booking section and the calendar loads.

Our web fonts are served by Google Fonts, which does not set cookies.

How to control cookies

  • Enable Global Privacy Control in your browser. This is the most complete option — it stops our analytics loading before any cookie is created, rather than deleting one afterwards.
  • Block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Every major browser allows this, under Privacy or Security settings. Blocking analytics cookies will not break anything on this site.
  • Opt out of Google Analytics specifically using Google's opt-out browser add-on.

Cookie settings are specific to each browser and device, so a choice made on your laptop will not carry across to your phone.

10. Your rights and how to use them

§ 541.051 — consumer rights · § 541.052 — how controllers must respond

We extend the rights below to every person who contacts us, wherever they live. It is simpler than applying different standards by state, and fairer.

  • Confirm and access — find out whether we are processing your personal data and obtain a copy of it.
  • Correct — have inaccurate personal data corrected, taking into account its nature and why we hold it.
  • Delete — have personal data you provided, or that we obtained about you, deleted.
  • Portability — receive your data in a portable and, to the extent technically feasible, readily usable format.
  • Opt out — of sale, targeted advertising and profiling with significant effects. We do none of these, but the right stands and we will honour a request as confirmation.
  • Appeal — challenge a refusal (see section 11).
  • Non-discrimination — we will not deny you service, charge you differently, or give you a lower standard of service because you exercised a right.

How to make a request

Email hello@wearelacuna.com with the word "Privacy" in the subject line, or write to us at the postal address in section 1. Both routes work equally.

You do not need to create an account to make a request, and we will never require one.

How we respond

  • We will acknowledge your request within 10 business days, so you know it reached us.
  • We will respond substantively within 45 days of receiving an authenticated request.
  • Where reasonably necessary we may take one further 45 days. If so, we will tell you within the first 45 days and explain why.
  • Responses are free, up to twice in any 12-month period. If a request is manifestly unfounded, excessive or repetitive, we may charge a reasonable administrative fee or decline it, and we will explain which and why.

Verifying who you are

Before we act on a request to access, correct, delete or port data, we take reasonable steps to confirm you are who you say you are. Usually that means replying from the email address we already hold for you, or confirming details already in our records. We will never ask for more information than we need, and we use anything you provide for verification only, then delete it.

If we cannot authenticate a request, we will tell you and explain what would allow us to proceed. We will not act on an unverified request, because doing so would risk disclosing your data to someone else.

Authorised agents

Someone may make a request on your behalf. We will ask for written authorisation signed by you, and we may also ask you to confirm the arrangement with us directly before we act.

California residents

California Civil Code § 1798.83 ("Shine the Light") lets California residents ask about personal data disclosed to third parties for those third parties' own direct marketing purposes. We make no such disclosures. You are welcome to email us to confirm that in writing.

Nevada residents

Nevada law gives residents the right to opt out of the sale of covered personal information. We do not sell personal information as Nevada defines it. To submit an opt-out anyway, email us with "Nevada opt-out" in the subject line.

11. Appeals

§ 541.053 — appeal process and referral to the Attorney General

If we decline your request, we will tell you why and how to appeal.

To appeal, reply to our decision or email hello@wearelacuna.com with "Privacy appeal" in the subject line, within 30 days of our decision. We will respond in writing within 60 days, explaining our reasoning.

If we deny the appeal, we will give you a method to contact the Texas Attorney General. You can also go directly to the Texas Attorney General's consumer privacy complaint page. Residents of other states may complain to their own Attorney General or supervisory authority.

Lacuna is established in the United Kingdom, so UK GDPR applies to its processing regardless of where you are located. The following therefore applies to everyone, not only to visitors in the UK or EU.

Additional rights: to object to processing based on legitimate interests; to restrict processing in certain circumstances; to withdraw consent at any time where processing relies on consent, without affecting prior processing; and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority — in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office.

International transfers: your correspondence is held in the United Kingdom, but several of the providers in section 6 — our website host, our analytics provider and our scheduling provider — are established in the United States. Where personal data is transferred out of the UK, we rely on the UK International Data Transfer Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, and, where the provider is certified under the UK Extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, on that certification. You can ask us for a copy of the safeguards in place for any named provider.

Response times under UK GDPR are one month, extendable by two further months for complex requests — shorter than the Texas timeline, so we apply whichever is sooner.

15. Security

§ 541.101(b)(2) — reasonable administrative, technical and physical data security practices

We maintain administrative, technical and physical safeguards appropriate to the volume and sensitivity of the data we hold. These include encryption of data in transit, access controls limiting who can view enquiry records, multi-factor authentication on the systems that store them, logging and review of access, and due diligence on the providers listed in section 6.

No transmission over the internet is completely secure and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please do not send passwords, payment details, or other confidential information through the website forms.

If a breach affects your personal data, we will notify you and the relevant regulators where the law requires it, within the applicable deadlines.

This is a business-to-business website, not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. Under Texas law the personal data of a child under 13 is sensitive data and requires verifiable parental consent, which we do not seek because we do not process it.

If you believe a child has given us personal data, email us and we will delete it promptly.
